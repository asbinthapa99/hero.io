About the Artist

Asbin Thapa

A creative soul bridging the gap between technology and art. Born in Nepal, now based in London, I explore the intersection of digital design, abstract expression, and visual storytelling.

As a Computer Science student at Ulster University with a focus on blockchain and cybersecurity, my art reflects the fusion of logic and creativity — structured yet free, technical yet emotional.

Every piece I create is an exploration of color, form, and meaning — driven by curiosity and a deep appreciation for beauty in both code and canvas.

Process

How I Create

From idea to final piece — a glimpse into the creative workflow

Ideation

Every piece starts with an idea — inspired by technology, nature, emotions, or the world around us.

Sketching

Rough concepts and compositions take shape through digital or traditional sketching and wireframing.

Creation

Using tools like Photoshop, Illustrator, and code, the vision comes to life with color, texture, and depth.

Refinement

Every detail is polished — from color grading to composition balance — until the piece feels complete.
